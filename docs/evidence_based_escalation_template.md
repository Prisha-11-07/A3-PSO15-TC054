## 7. Limitations
A failed ping alone does not prove that a service is unavailable.
High latency does not by itself confirm congestion.
These tests cannot independently prove a damaged cable, Wi-Fi interference,
or a fault inside the ISP network.

## 8. Evidence Classification
Mark every result as LIVE MEASUREMENT or SIMULATED.
Never present simulated values as real network measurements.
